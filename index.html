Kashi

Gold on silk, one shuttle at a time.

Banarasi cloth is still made on pit looms: silk warp, zari weft, and a design that may take weeks to climb the pallu. Sabhyakashi works with that tradition — katan for weight, kadhua for motif, jangla for the garden that never repeats the same vine twice.

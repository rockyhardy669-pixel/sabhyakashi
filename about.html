Sabhyakashi

A house for Banarasi cloth.

Sabhyakashi is a store of traditional Banarasi sarees — katan, kadhua, jangla, tanchoi, tissue, georgette, and shikargah — made in Varanasi and sold with the names the weavers already use.

The city is Kashi. The cloth is silk and zari. We keep that language on the site so a bride, a mother, or a merchant can find the weave they came for, then buy it through a shop that later runs on Shopify: stock, price, and orders in one admin.

What we mean by traditional

A Banarasi saree is not a print on mill fabric. It is a design tied on the loom: gold or tested zari, a pallu that is a second composition, a blouse piece cut from the same warp. Kadhua takes longer because each motif is built on its own. Jangla covers the field in a garden. Shikargah tells a hunt. Those words stay on our tags.

When this site moves to Shopify, the merchant will add real pieces, real metres, and real photos. The pages you see now are the look of the house.

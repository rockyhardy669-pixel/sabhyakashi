Sabhyakashi

Rooted in Kashi. Worn with grace.

Sabhyakashi celebrates the colour, detail, and cultural richness of traditional Banarasi sarees. Our name brings together a sense of refinement and the enduring spirit of Kashi.

Our catalogue is a place to explore sarees by their patterns, colours, and character—whether you are drawn to a floral jangla, a delicate buti, or the subtle texture of tanchoi.

What we mean by traditional

Banarasi textile traditions span many materials, techniques, and motifs. Kadhua describes a weaving technique, jangla is associated with flowing floral patterns, and shikargah features narrative motifs. Each piece deserves its own clear description.

We are building our first collection. Current listings and prices are samples; confirmed photographs, material details, and availability will accompany our own catalogue.
